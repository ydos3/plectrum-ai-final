# Security Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

Security reports can be sent to: plectrummusic3@gmail.com

Please include a clear description, affected URL or file path, reproduction steps, impact, and your contact information. Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before Plectrum has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

Do not submit real user recordings, secrets, private keys, or personal data in reports unless strictly necessary. Plectrum will make reasonable efforts to review and address security reports for active demo/private beta deployments.

Secrets such as API keys, Supabase service keys, Gemini keys, YouTube keys, Razorpay keys, passwords, and tokens must not be committed to the repository.
