# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plectrum AI may process information when you use Plectrum AI.

## What Plectrum AI Is

Plectrum AI is an AI-assisted guitar/music practice companion for educational, personal, and private practice use.

## Data We May Process

Depending on how you use the app, Plectrum AI may process profile information, app preferences, saved songs, practice content, AI prompts and outputs, device/browser information, logs, recordings created in the Practice Room, and information needed to operate embedded third-party content.

The app may use browser `localStorage` for saved app state, songs, language choices, and generated practice content. It may use IndexedDB for private practice recordings. These browser stores remain on your device unless you clear them or the app later adds account sync.

## Recordings

Recordings are intended for private practice. If recordings are stored locally in your browser, they may remain until you delete them, clear browser data, or uninstall/reset the app. Do not record, upload, publish, or distribute content unless you have the necessary rights and permissions.

## AI Providers

Some features may send prompts, song requests, images, or generated content to AI providers such as Gemini/Google. Do not submit sensitive personal information or content you are not authorized to process.

## Third-Party Services

Plectrum AI may interact with services such as YouTube embeds, LRCLIB, Gemini/Google, Supabase, Vercel, Razorpay, analytics providers, or similar infrastructure providers, depending on deployment and enabled features. These services may process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

## IP, Location, Analytics, and Cookies

The app may process IP-derived location or timezone information to personalize the studio experience. Analytics or performance tools may process device, browser, usage, and approximate location data. Third-party embeds may use cookies or similar technologies controlled by those platforms.

## Retention

Local browser data remains until deleted by the user or browser. Server-side logs, if enabled, should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for security, debugging, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.

## Your Requests

To request access, correction, deletion, or export of personal data, contact plectrummusic3@gmail.com. We may need information to verify your request.

## Children

Plectrum AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.
