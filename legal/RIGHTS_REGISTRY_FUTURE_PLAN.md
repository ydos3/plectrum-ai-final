# Rights Registry Future Plan

This is documentation only. It is not wired into the current app and does not change existing product behavior.

Future licensing and rights metadata could include:

- `song_id`
- `title`
- `artist`
- `source`
- `license_status`
- `allowed_context`
- `territory`
- `rights_holder`
- `takedown_status`
- `last_reviewed_at`

Potential future `license_status` values could include `licensed`, `user_provided`, `public_domain`, `metadata_only`, `unverified`, and `blocked`.

Potential future `allowed_context` values could include `private_practice`, `educational_estimate`, `metadata_display`, `full_display`, and `public_distribution`.

This future registry should be reviewed with music licensing counsel before implementation.
