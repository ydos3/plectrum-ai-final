Security Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

Security reports can be sent to plectrummusic3@gmail.com. Please include a clear description, affected URL or file path, reproduction steps, impact, and your contact information.

Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities before Plectrum AI has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate. Do not submit real user recordings, secrets, private keys, or personal data unless strictly necessary.

Secrets such as API keys, Supabase service keys, Gemini keys, YouTube keys, Razorpay keys, passwords, and tokens must not be committed to the repository.
